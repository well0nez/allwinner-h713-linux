Keystone

Keystone makes the projected picture square on the wall, however the projector stands. Drag a corner, or move the picked corner with the arrows.

Step per press, in per-mille of the panel (1 fine, 10 medium, 50 coarse).

What is the mask? What does "Auto keystone now" do?

The calibration mask puts a test picture on the wall (grey field, frame, circle) so you can aim a corner with no source plugged in. "Auto keystone now" reads the accelerometer (tilt sensor) and sets the keystone once for the tilt the projector has right now.

Projection mode

How the projector is installed (the vendor's installation mode): front, rear, ceiling or rear ceiling. Rear mirrors the picture left-right, ceiling turns it over; the corner names above stay as the audience sees them in every mode.

After a move

What the projector does once after it has been moved and stands still again: nothing, the automatic keystone, or the keystone and then the autofocus. It uses the accelerometer - and, for the autofocus, the camera and the focus motor.

Level reference

What is a level reference?

The automatic keystone already corrects against the ideal level pose (0, 0, 1 g), shown as ref ideal in the status strip. Put the projector on a surface a spirit level has passed and press the button: it stores the pose the sensor reads there, which refines the ideal by the sensor's own mounting offset - the status strip then says ref set. ref refused means the stored file no longer matches the axis map in keystone.conf; the ideal applies until the button is pressed again.

Focus

Manual focus: moves the focus motor in microsteps. Try the small ones first; the motor is slow on purpose.

Expert

Picture

The picture controls: brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, sharpness and the noise reduction and contrast enhancements.

Preset

A preset is the vendor's picture mode: one tap sets the mode and the nine controls at once, and its colour temperature unless you set one in the Colour tab. Use cinema for movies, game for consoles.

Aspect

Aspect ratio: how the source is fitted onto the panel. Leave it on auto unless the picture is distorted.

What exactly is saved? What does brightness do?

Save writes the nine values, the preset, the gamma, the colour temperature, the aspect and the zoom to the device, so they come back after a reboot. Nothing here is written until you press it. Brightness is the black level on this hardware, not a lamp control - on bright content you will see nothing move; the lamp is not adjustable.

Wi-Fi

Where the projector gets its network. A change goes live at once - if you lose the connection, the old settings come back by themselves after 120 s.

Why do I have to keep the settings by hand?

On the access point the network goes away under the browser the moment the new settings apply, so the page cannot be sure to hear your answer. That is why the change is on trial: press "Keep these settings" in the yellow banner while you still see the page, or the tool puts the previous settings back.
